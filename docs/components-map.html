Why it's shaped this way

  • AI reads, code decides. Gemini only reports what the caller said (step 7). The five gates are plain code (step 8), so a persuasive caller can't talk the model past a rule.
  • No price ever leaves the system. Price figures are kept out of Meher's brief (step 3), the website agent's replies are filtered (4b), and every transcript is scanned after the call (6).
  • Two front doors, one brain. Phone and website calls meet at step 6 and get identical treatment.
  • A person exactly where judgment is needed. Designers own pricing and the consultation; Nikhil owns complaints; the front desk owns call-backs and one-question follow-ups (step 10).
  • Gmail, flagged by subject line. Each lead is its own searchable thread; a filter on [Aangan marks them important so the phone notifies.

Not in scope for this build: WhatsApp and the web form. They would enter at step 6 with the same gates.
